Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

AlgoMirror is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we access: your public profile information, your video list and public metrics (views, likes, shares, comments).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to generate algorithm analysis insights within the app. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage

We do not permanently store your TikTok data. Analysis is performed in real time and data is not retained after your session ends.

4. TikTok API

Our integration with TikTok follows TikTok's Platform Policy. Access tokens are stored only in your browser session and never on our servers.

5. Your Rights

You can revoke AlgoMirror's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's app settings under "Manage app permissions".

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at github.com/Cfarialopes/algomirror
